# Changelog

## November 2026: partnership rules and presentation

- New partnership rules engine. Titles come only from a package the partner chooses; totals never unlock titles. Three tracks (program, season, institutional) plus an unrestricted gift with no title. Every benefit has its own scope and stacking rule.
- The builder now asks before combining or replacing program selections with a season package, separates money from titles and benefits, labels proposed support as not raised, and sends only unrestricted gifts to online checkout. Designated support is confirmed first so designations are recorded.
- Saved proposals hold no contact details, expire after 7 days and clear when selections change.
- Presentation rebuilt as 12 stations with the train as the slide transition, sound only on intentional moves, a station list, restart, presenter view and an offline PDF.
- Fixed: stretched empty space in the demand section, cut-off content under the fixed controls, tall cropped photos in program details, underlines on wrapped headlines, and a headline styling bug that italicized single words.
- Automated tests added in `tools/`.

## November 2026: institutional readiness update

- Partnership journeys for nine kinds of institutions, and partnership levels refocused on engagement rather than logos.
- A one-page overview (HTML and PDF), an updated prospectus and an updated presentation.
- Essential content is now written into the static HTML, so the site is readable without JavaScript.
