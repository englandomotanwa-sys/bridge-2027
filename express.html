EXPartner Express · about 90 seconds

A student-powered infrastructure for opportunity in Washington, DC.

BRIDGE the District develops college students into community leaders, then uses their closeness to younger students to open doors to the people, institutions and experiences that make opportunity real. Opportunity should not depend on who you already know.

Executive summary

  • What: a student-led 501(c)(3) in Washington, DC, closing opportunity gaps for youth within and beyond the classroom.
  • How: college students train as Impact Scholars, then design and lead career, civic and community programs, and bring younger students along.
  • Demand: .
  • Spring 2027: 10 planned programs, January to April, with a $10,000 season goal.
  • The ask: partnerships from $250 to $10,000+, unrestricted or program-specific, plus mentors, volunteers and in-kind support.

Why it exists

DC is full of open doors. Not every student gets to walk through them.

    Who it serves

    College students
    Impact Scholars from Howard, American, George Washington and Georgetown universities, who gain leadership experience, professional exposure, mentors and networks.
    Younger students
    Students in DC schools who gain enrichment, college exposure, mentorship and resources, led by near-peers.
    The community
    Professionals, schools and institutions who meet students as colleagues, not an audience.

    What makes the model different

    For students. By students. With students.
