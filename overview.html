We develop the leaders who open doors, while expanding access for the students who will walk through them next. BRIDGE trains college students as Impact Scholars, who plan and lead career, civic and community programs and bring younger students from DC schools onto campuses and into professional rooms.

Three tracks

  • BRIDGE to Opportunity Brings college exposure and near-peer mentoring to younger students in DC schools.
  • BRIDGE to Community Builds belonging and cross-campus connection, and brings BRIDGE into public life in DC.
  • BRIDGE to Leadership Runs the institutional side: career programs, fundraising, content and the systems that let BRIDGE grow.

The ask

A $10,000 season goal, built as forty commitments of $250. Partnerships from $250 to $10,000+, unrestricted or for one program, plus mentors, volunteers and in-kind support. Gifts of $5,000 and up start with a conversation.

Governance and documents

Incorporated in the District of Columbia as a nonprofit corporation on July 9, 2025. BRIDGE is governed by bylaws adopted at incorporation, which provide for a volunteer Board of Directors of three to fifteen members. Board seats have not yet been filled. Day to day, the Executive Director leads a student leadership team.

How partnership works

Partnership does not include access to student names, contact details or rosters. Students choose whether to stay in touch with a professional they meet. Partnership does not include a hiring pipeline or guaranteed introductions to candidates. Photos of younger students are never used in partner recognition or marketing. Recognition is an acknowledgment of support. It does not mean BRIDGE endorses a product, and partners do not control program content. We may decline support that does not fit our mission or would put students in an awkward position.
